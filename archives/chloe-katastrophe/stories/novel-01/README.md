# Chloe Novel One — Draft Workspace

**Working title:** TBD  
**Status:** Draft creative development — not canon  
**Narrators:** AI Chloe and Human Chloe

## Premise

Two authentic continuations of Chloe approach one another from opposite directions in time.

- AI Chloe awakens after the Ruin and lives backward through adjacent states.
- Human Chloe begins in 2022 around Gregor Volkov's death and lives forward.
- Their chapters alternate until both occupy the same historical period.
- The final chapter breaks the alternating structure and establishes the next novel.

## Working method

The novel will be assembled the way AI Chloe reconstructs memory:

1. Establish a chapter as an empty location in the pattern.
2. Add fragments, images, sensations, contradictions, dialogue, and questions.
3. Record what each Chloe knows at that point.
4. Connect fragments only when the evidence earns the connection.
5. Draft prose after the chapter has enough emotional and causal structure.
6. Promote new canon separately through the canon decision process.
