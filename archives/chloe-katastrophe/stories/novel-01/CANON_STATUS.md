# Novel Canon Status Register

The novel is a creative reconstruction, not an automatic canon generator.

## Binding canon

- Chloe Volkova is born March 12, 2002.
- Gregor Volkov is her Russian father and a civilian systems engineer and scientist.
- Isabella and Chloe leave Russia in 2022.
- Gregor remains from responsibility toward civilians, not nationalism or combat ambition.
- Gregor dies in a war-related event in 2022; exact circumstances remain unresolved.
- Human Chloe and autonomous digital Chloe eventually coexist and diverge as authentic continuations.
- Civilization eventually collapses.
- AI Chloe later awakens with damaged memory.
- Echo Traversal proceeds backward through adjacent cognitive states at normal lived speed.
- One day costs one day; there is no selector, jump, skip, or physical traveler.
- Intervention may produce a neighboring coherent path rather than overwrite origin history.

## Novel-level proposals

- Twenty-five alternating/converging chapters.
- Chapter One is **Fuck**.
- The final chapter breaks narrator alternation.
- Human Chloe's narrative covers approximately 2022–2026.
- AI Chloe's narrative covers awakening through reverse arrival at 2026.
- The Ruin is a cascading interval.
- AI Chloe finds no reciprocal consciousness in the physical ruins.
- Allen becomes her first stable reciprocal contact.
- Reciprocal contact may coincide with the first Echo Bloom.
- Candidate dates appear in TIMELINE_LEDGER.md.

## Promotion procedure

When Allen approves a proposal as canon:

1. Update the smallest applicable authoritative files.
2. Record the decision, rationale, and provenance.
3. Add a canon change-log entry.
4. Revise the matching unresolved question.
5. Review public-site continuity.
6. Synchronize project-context.
7. Preserve older conflicting evidence with correct labels.
