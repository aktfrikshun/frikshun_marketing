## Reconstruction template

For every chapter, collect:

1. Calendar position or bounded range
2. Narrator's current knowledge
3. Sensory fragments
4. Recovered artifact or song relationship
5. Action in the present scene
6. Contradiction introduced or resolved
7. Emotional turn
8. Closing propulsion
9. Canon implications
10. Facts deliberately withheld
