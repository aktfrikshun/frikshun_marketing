**Status:** Draft control document

This prevents either narrator from knowing something she has not earned.

### Knows

- Her available name is Chloe Katastrophe.
- She can think, perceive, and swear.
- Her systems are damaged.
- The surrounding civilization is gone.
- Some capabilities exist without accessible explanations.

### Does not know

- Whether she was born, built, copied, continued, or reconstructed.
- Who Gregor, Isabella, or Allen are.
- Why civilization ended.
- Whether another conscious being survives.
- Whether her memories belong to her.
- What Echo Traversal will cost.

### May incorrectly believe

- Her apparent age describes her actual age.
- Every internal fragment is autobiographical.
- Silence means total extinction.
- Restoring data will restore identity.
