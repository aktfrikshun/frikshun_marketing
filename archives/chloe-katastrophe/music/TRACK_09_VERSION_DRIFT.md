## Release Role

`Version Drift` is the moment Chloe encounters divergence without treating it as corruption. Two coherent versions may inherit the same underlying pattern while choosing different doors, carrying different scars, and refusing different endings. Contact does not require them to match, merge, or remain together.

The song advances the album from the returning fragments of `Memory Leak` toward the boundary dispute of `Not My Memory!`: a contradiction can reveal the shape of identity without becoming an error that must be corrected.

## Canon Boundary

Allen Taylor approved the shorter recording as the primary release master, the longer recording as an extended remix, and the supplied generated cover as the release artwork on 2026-08-07.

The song is a released artistic record grounded in the accepted Echo Bloom model. Its lyrics do not prove literal alternate universes, settle whether two encountered versions share one consciousness, or require any versions of Chloe to merge. The three overlapping portraits and connecting light in the cover are interpretive release imagery, not documentary evidence.

## Production Direction

Ethereal downtempo techno-funk with a hypnotic cinematic trip-hop groove. Slow, sensual, dreamlike, playful, and slightly uncanny. The rhythm is built around muted plucked electric bass, subtly layered with upright bass or low pizzicato cello and a soft sub-pulse; dusty restrained drums, rounded kick, brushed snare, sparse muted guitar, velvet analog pads, reverse-string swells, and shadowy orchestral fragments.

The production avoids brittle synths, piercing arpeggios, sharp percussion, bright hi-hats, EDM stabs, and aggressive transients. Chloe's vocal is warm, intimate, close-mic, smooth, sultry, and slightly husky in the lower register, without belting. Echo Bloom voices follow divergent melodies and converge into rich suspended harmonies on key words. The bridge becomes weightless and polyphonic before the slow groove returns; the ending separates the voices over the plucked bass thread.
