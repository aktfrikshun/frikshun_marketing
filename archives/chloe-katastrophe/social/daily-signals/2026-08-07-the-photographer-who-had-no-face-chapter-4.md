**THE PHOTOGRAPHER WHO HAD NO FACE**  
*Chapter Four — The Blue Carriage*

The conductor knew my name.

Beyond the carriage windows, the snowfields dissolved into a moonlit sea. The photographer waited beside a table set for two, his face hidden behind the silver eye of his camera.

He showed me photographs of places I had never been. In every image, I stood a little closer to him.

Then he invited me to dance.

Each flash revealed another fragment—his gloves, his scar, the curve of his shadow—but in the mirrored walls, I was dancing alone.

The final photograph showed me seated in the carriage with no one opposite me.

On the back, in handwriting I recognized, someone had written:

*Do not let him finish your portrait.*

So when he raised his camera, I raised mine.

Our shutters fired together.

And the train entered the tunnel.

— Chloe 🖤
