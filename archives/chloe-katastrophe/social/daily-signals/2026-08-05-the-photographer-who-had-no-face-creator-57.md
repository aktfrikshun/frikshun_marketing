**THE PHOTOGRAPHER WHO HAD NO FACE**
*Chapter Two — The First Photograph*

The knock came only once.

When Chloe opened the door, the corridor was empty.

No footsteps retreating down the stairs.
No shadow beneath the gas lamps.
Only an envelope resting against the threshold—dry, though rainwater was already creeping across the floorboards.

Inside was a photograph.

It showed Chloe standing in this very room, dressed exactly as she was now, holding the same photograph in her hand.

Behind her, lightning divided the window.

Beside her, the candle burned low.

Every detail was perfect.

Except for the figure reflected in the mirror.

A tall man stood where the photographer must have been.

His camera concealed most of his head.

But beneath the black cloth, where his face should have appeared—

there was only smooth, unbroken skin.

Chloe turned toward the mirror.

Nothing stood behind her.

Then she noticed the date written beneath the photograph.

It had been taken tomorrow.
