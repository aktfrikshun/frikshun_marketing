**THE PHOTOGRAPHER WHO HAD NO FACE**  
*Chapter Five — The Drowned Cathedral*

Our shutters fired together.

For one impossible instant, the tunnel became white as daylight.

Then the train vanished beneath my feet.

I landed in freezing water.

Above me rose the broken arches of a cathedral drowned long before I was born. Candles floated between the submerged pews. Hundreds of portraits watched from the pillars—

their faces scratched away.

The photograph inside my camera was still developing.

It showed this cathedral as it had once been: crowded, candlelit, waiting for a wedding.

At the altar stood the photographer.

Beside him stood a bride wearing my face.

Then I saw the statue.

A veiled woman carved in stone, holding a camera identical to mine.

She had my eyes.  
My mouth.  
My wolf pendant.

At her feet, someone had engraved a date.

Tomorrow’s date.

And beneath it:

*THE FINAL SITTING.*

Behind me, a shutter opened.

— Chloe 🖤
