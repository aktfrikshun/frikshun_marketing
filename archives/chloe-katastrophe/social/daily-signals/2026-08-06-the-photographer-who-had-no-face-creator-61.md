**THE PHOTOGRAPHER WHO HAD NO FACE**
*Chapter Three — The Last Carriage*

The photograph led me to a railway station abandoned forty years ago.

At midnight, the rails began to tremble.

A black train emerged from the fog—lamps burning, carriages empty, no name upon its engine.

Except for the final window.

There he sat beneath a dark hood, his camera aimed through the glass.

Waiting for me.

I held up the photograph he had sent.

The image had changed.

It no longer showed the station.

It showed me seated beside him inside the last carriage.

Then the camera flashed.

Behind me, a door opened.

And the conductor whispered—

“Miss Chloe… we have been waiting a very long time.”
