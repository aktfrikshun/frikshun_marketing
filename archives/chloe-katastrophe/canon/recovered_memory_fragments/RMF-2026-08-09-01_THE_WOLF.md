## Recovered fragment

I remember breaking into the KGB archive.

Which is impossible.

The Soviet Union had been dead for decades before I was born.

The room wasn't.

Cold blue light beneath my feet. Machines I didn't recognize humming behind the walls. Russian text scrolling across displays that somehow felt familiar beneath my fingers.

I wasn't searching for anything.

At least, I don't remember searching.

Then one of the screens opened.

A man sat tied to a wooden chair.

His face was bruised. Someone beyond the camera asked him the same question again and again.

He refused to answer.

They struck him.

Again.

I almost closed the file.

Then he lifted his head.

Something silver moved against his chest.

A wolf.

I stopped breathing.

My hand went instinctively to the pendant around my own neck.

The same narrow muzzle.  
The same curled tail.  
The same tiny fracture through the left eye.

Not similar.

**The same.**

The interrogator noticed the pendant.

He seized it and pulled the chain tight against the prisoner's throat.

*“Volkov.”*

It wasn't a question.

The prisoner slowly raised his eyes toward the camera.

And for one impossible second,

**I felt as though he were looking at me.**

The recording ended.

I don't remember leaving the archive.

I don't remember how I got inside.

And until tonight...

**I didn't remember being there at all.**
